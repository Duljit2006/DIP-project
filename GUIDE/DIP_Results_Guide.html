DIP Project Guide • Where to See Your Results

Where and How You Will See Your Results

Whenever you run your Python files, you don't have to wonder if they worked. You will see your outputs in two simple, familiar places:

✨ The 2 Main Places to Look:

  • 1. In your Terminal / Command Prompt: Clear text, status messages, and exact score numbers (PSNR in dB and SSIM) print right on your screen.
  • 2. On your Screen & in the results/ Folder: Interactive comparison windows pop up right in front of you, and ready-to-use PNG image files are automatically saved for your presentation slides!

1. When You Run src/dct_utils.py (Stage 1)

Chpping Images into 8×8 Blocks & Erasing Frequencies

How to Run: Open your terminal in the project folder and type:

python src/dct_utils.py
📟 Output 1: In Your Terminal
Terminal - Verification Output
Loading image: datasets/Set5/img_001_SRF_2.png
Image original shape: (512, 512)
Total 8x8 blocks extracted: 4,096 blocks
High frequencies truncated: First 15 preserved, 49 erased to 0.0
Reconstructed image pixel range: min = 0.0, max = 1.0
✔ Success: Slicing, DCT, Truncation, and IDCT all working cleanly!
🪟 Output 2: Interactive Pop-up Window

A window pops up on your screen comparing the original image with the blurry, blocky truncated image so you can visually verify the compression effect!

Figure 1: What Your Pop-up Window Looks Like on Screen

2. When You Run src/metrics.py (Stage 2 - Baseline Score)

Measuring How Bad the Blurry Baseline Is

How to Run: In your terminal, type:

python src/metrics.py
📟 Output: In Your Terminal

The script evaluates the images in Set5 and immediately prints exact baseline numbers:

Terminal - Baseline Scores
============================================================
BASELINE EVALUATION ON SET5 (Zero-Padded IDCT)
============================================================
baby.png: PSNR = 26.42 dB, SSIM = 0.8120
bird.png: PSNR = 25.10 dB, SSIM = 0.7845
butterfly.png: PSNR = 23.85 dB, SSIM = 0.7230
head.png: PSNR = 27.05 dB, SSIM = 0.8315
woman.png: PSNR = 24.28 dB, SSIM = 0.7650
------------------------------------------------------------
Average Baseline PSNR: 25.34 dB | Average SSIM: 0.7832
============================================================

💡 Save these numbers! This is the benchmark score that the GNN needs to beat!

3. When You Run evaluate.py (Stage 5 - The Final Benchmark)

The Victory Lap: Seeing the AI Outperform the Baseline!

Once the model is trained and you place model.pth in the checkpoints/ folder, you run:

python evaluate.py
📟 Output 1: In Your Terminal (Score Gain)
Terminal - Model vs Baseline Benchmark
============================================================
EVALUATION ON SET5: BASELINE VS GNN
============================================================
baby.png:
  Baseline PSNR: 26.42 dB | Baseline SSIM: 0.8120
  GNN PSNR: 28.75 dB | GNN SSIM: 0.8540
  → PSNR Gain: +2.33 dB! (Clear improvement)

bird.png:
  Baseline PSNR: 25.10 dB | Baseline SSIM: 0.7845
  GNN PSNR: 27.22 dB | GNN SSIM: 0.8260
  → PSNR Gain: +2.12 dB!
------------------------------------------------------------
🎉 Average PSNR Gain Over Baseline: +2.18 dB!
============================================================
🪟 Output 2: Interactive Pop-up Window on Your Screen

Because we added plt.show(), a large 3-panel window will automatically pop up right in front of you for each image:

Figure 2: The 3-Panel Visual Comparison Pop-up Window

You can zoom in on the picture using your mouse, inspect the details, and close the window whenever you are ready for the next image!

📁 Output 3: Saved in Your results/ Folder

The script also saves all of these images directly to your computer so you can drag-and-drop them into your PowerPoint slides:
